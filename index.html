PhD Candidate in Economics

Sofía Balladares

University of Barcelona · Institut d'Economia de Barcelona (IEB)

I am a PhD candidate in Economics at the University of Barcelona and an affiliated researcher at the Institut d'Economia de Barcelona (IEB), supervised by Dirk Foremny and Miguel Almunia. My research is in public economics: I study inequality, tax design and tax compliance. I am currently working on the incidence of personal and corporate taxes and on how taxation affects firms' automation decisions.

Before my PhD, I worked as a research assistant at the EU Tax Observatory at the Paris School of Economics and in the Public Sector and Fiscal Policy Unit of the Banco de España. During my PhD I have been on research visits at the Paris School of Economics and the Institute for Fiscal Studies.

Featured publication

Published

Fiscal Drag with Microsimulation: Evidence from Spanish Tax Records

with Esteban García-Miralles

Fiscal Studies, 47(2): 113–137, 2026

Fiscal drag arises when nominal tax parameters remain unchanged despite nominal income growth, thereby increasing effective tax rates and revenue. We use Spanish administrative tax records and a detailed microsimulation model to examine fiscal drag in personal income taxation. We uncover significant heterogeneity in tax-to-base elasticities across income sources and the income distribution, and our counterfactual simulations show that fiscal drag accounts for about a third of revenue growth from 2019 to 2023.
